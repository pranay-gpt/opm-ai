If the AIM solution method is being used, this keyword may be used to enforce a minimum reservoir pore volume below which a cell is always solved implicitly. This can be useful for radial studies, in which inner grid blocks, near the well, may be better locked in implicit mode.

The AIMPVI keyword may be specified more than once in the SCHEDULE section.

UNITS: rm3 (METRIC), rb (FIELD), rcc (LAB), rm3 (PVT-M)
