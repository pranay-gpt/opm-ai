This keyword turns on the CO2 storage option. The mutual solubility and fluid properties of CO2 and H2O are considered, where the H2O rich phase might include salts.

CO2STORE is the only keyword required for this option.

For further information see "CO2 storage" in the ECLIPSE Technical Description.
