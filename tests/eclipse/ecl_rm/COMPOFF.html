The COMPOFF keyword switches off all automatic compressors defined by the GASFCOMP keyword in the Gas Field Operations Model, or the NETCOMPA keyword in the Extended Network Model, except those defined to stay on permanently (PERM in item 9 of NETCOMPA). The compressors will be turned on again automatically as soon as they are needed.

This keyword has no effect on compressors defined with the GNETPUMP keyword.

See "Gas field operations model" and "Network option" in the ECLIPSE Technical Description for further details.

The keyword has no associated data.
