This keyword specifies a gas phase diffusion coefficient for each component in a compositional run. These are used to define diffusive flows in terms of vapor mole fractions.

The normal diffusion coefficients input using by DIFFCGAS are defined by the condition (refer to Reid, Prausnitz and Polling (1987)):

Ji=-c Di ⁡∂ yi ∂d [Eq. 247.1]

where

c is the total molar concentration.
J i is the flux of component i per unit area.
D i is the diffusion coefficient of component i.
∂yi∂d is molar concentration gradient of component i.

These diffusion coefficients are used in ECLIPSE 300 to obtain gas interblock diffusive flows, which take the form:

F igdiff =TD Dig (Sgbgm )Δyi [Eq. 247.2]

where

T D is the diffusivity, the analogue of transmissibility for diffusive flow
y i are vapor mole fractions.

For more details, see "Diffusion" in the ECLIPSE Technical Description.

Diffusivity should be entered in the appropriate unit set. If the values available are in cm2/s, they may be converted as follows:

LAB units: 1 cm2/s = 3600 cm2/hr

METRIC or PVT-M units: 1 cm2/s = 8.64 m2/day

FIELD units: 1 cm2/s = 92.9979 ft2/day

UNITS: m2/day (METRIC), ft2/day (FIELD), cm2/hr (LAB), m2/day (PVT-M)

DEFAULT: 0.0
