This keyword specifies water-gas cross phase diffusion coefficient for each component in a compositional CO2STORE or GASWAT run. These are used to define cross phase molecular diffusion from a cell containing water to a cell containing gas. The opposite diffusive flow from a cell containing gas to a cell containing water should be specified by the keyword DIFFCGW.

The potential is constructed by mixing the water composition and the gas composition. The potential is then determined by using the liquid and or vapor mole fraction of the mixed cell.

For more details, see "Diffusion" in the ECLIPSE Technical Description.

Diffusivity should be entered in the appropriate unit set. If the values available are in cm2/s, they may be converted as follows:

LAB units: 1 cm2/s = 3600 cm2/hr

METRIC or PVT-M units: 1 cm2/s = 8.64 m2/day

FIELD units: 1 cm2/s = 92.9979 ft2/day

UNITS: m2/day (METRIC), ft2/day (FIELD), cm2/hr (LAB), m2/day (PVT-M)

DEFAULT: 0.0

Example

DIFFCWG
--For      CO2    H2O    (field units)
           0.16   0.15    /
