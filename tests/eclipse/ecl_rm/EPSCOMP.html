The EPSCOMP keyword specifies the component with which to perform compositional dependent end-point scaling. The nominated component is subsequently used to interpolate the ENKRVC, ENPCVC and ENPTVC tables.

The data comprises NTSFUN (set in the TABDIMS keyword) each terminated by a slash (/).

Example

With NTSFUN=1, and specifying end-point scaling dependence on the first hydrocarbon component.

EPSCOMP
 1 /
