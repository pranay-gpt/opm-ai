If this keyword is entered in the RUNSPEC section, it is possible to specify directions of penetration other than Z in the in the WELLCOMP keyword (item 12) or the COMPDAT keyword (item 13).

For the X direction of penetration, the Peaceman formula for the well completion (discussed in "The connection transmissibility factor" in the ECLIPSE Technical Description) is still used, but with Kx replacing Kz and the cell dimensions in the y-z directions being used to obtain the equivalent radius. Note that gravity effects are not included.

This keyword has no associated data.

This keyword is not required in ECLIPSE 100.
