The KRODI keyword specifies the molar density of individual components at which damage initiates, see the KRODM.

The data comprises NTSFUN (set in the TABDIMS keyword) rows of data, each terminated by a slash (/).

Each row consists of N entries, where N = number of hydrocarbon components + the number of water components.

UNITS: kg-M/rm3 (METRIC), lb-M/rb (FIELD), gm-M/rcc (LAB), kg-M/rm3 (PVT-M)

Example

With NTSFUN = 2, 6 hydrocarbon components and 1 water component. Damage occurring due to the presence of the sixth hydrocarbon component at concentrations greater than 0.5.

KRODI
