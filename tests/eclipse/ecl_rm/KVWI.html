This keyword can only be used with the THERMAL option.

For each oil component, the oil component K-value is fitted according to the equation

K(P,T)=e 5.372697⋅(1 +Ac)⋅( 1-T crit /T) ⋅P crit /P [Eq. 247.1]

where

T is the temperature (K (METRIC), °R (FIELD), K (LAB), K (PVT-M))
P  is the pressure (barsa (METRIC), psia (FIELD), atma (LAB), atma (PVT-M))
A c is the acentric factor for each component as entered with keyword ACF
T crit is the component critical temperature as entered with keyword TCRIT
P crit  is the component critical pressure as entered with keyword PCRIT.

This keyword requires acentric factors with keyword ACF, critical temperatures and pressures with keywords TCRIT and PCRIT.

There is no data for this keyword.
