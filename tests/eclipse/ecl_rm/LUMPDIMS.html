The data consists of two items, describing the numbers of lumped and detailed components (see "Delumping and lumping" in the ECLIPSE Technical Description). The data must be terminated by a slash (/).

  1. The number of lumped components
  2. The maximum number of detailed components per lumped component.

Example

Five lumped components; each having six detailed components

LUMPDIMS
 5   6  /
