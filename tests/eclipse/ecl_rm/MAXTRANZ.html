The keyword should be followed by a single positive real number, the maximum allowed z-transmissibility between two cells. Where calculated values are larger than this value, they are reduced.

UNITS: cP.rm3/day/bars (METRIC), cP.rb/day/psi (FIELD), cP.rcc/hr/atm (LAB), cP.rm3/day/atm (PVT-M)

DEFAULT: 1.0E20 (all unit sets)
