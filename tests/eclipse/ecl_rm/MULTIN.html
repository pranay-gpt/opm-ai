This indicates that input files, which may be either multiple or unified, are to be multiple. This is the default; unified files may be requested with the UNIFIN keyword.

Multiple files enable the progress of the run to be monitored, and are generally preferred; but they do produce a larger number of files, which may be inconvenient on machines without a directory structure.

This keyword has no associated data.
