This keyword selects a nine-point transmissibility option which adds diagonal transmissibility values to the grid as non-neighbor connections in the YZ plane. The method of obtaining these extra transmissibility values is described in "Nine-point schemes" in the ECLIPSE Technical Description. The total y- and z-transmissibilities over the grid are preserved, but the addition of the diagonal terms may limit grid orientation effects when fluids are injected at adverse mobility ratios.

The keyword has no associated data.
