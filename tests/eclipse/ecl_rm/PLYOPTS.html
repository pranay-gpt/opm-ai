This keyword sets optional parameters for the ECLIPSE 300 polymer model. See the "Polymer flood model" in the ECLIPSE Technical Description for further information.

  1. The minimum water saturation for polymer effects.

    DEFAULT: 1e-6

    For cells with small water saturations, a small amount of polymer will give a high concentration of polymer in the water phase, which could have a large effect on the water phase viscosity. To prevent numerical errors effecting the solution, polymer concentrations are disregarded for cells where the minimum water saturation is below a threshold given by this item.
