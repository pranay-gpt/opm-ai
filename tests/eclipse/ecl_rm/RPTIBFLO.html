This keyword controls whether the interblock flows (both surface and reservoir flows) reported in the restart files are the values at the restart time or an average calculated between the previous restart (or the beginning of the run) and the current restart. The keyword does not alter the calculation of interblock flows used internally in the simulator.

This keyword is followed by a single record terminated by a slash (/). The record consists of one item of data:

  1. The nature of the reported interblock flows:
    CURRENT The flows at the time when the restart is written.
    AVERAGE The average of the flows calculated between restarts.

    DEFAULT: CURRENT

End data record with a slash (/).

Notes

  • The average interblock flow is the cumulative interblock flow between restarts divided by the time between restarts.
  • The cumulative flow is the sum of the flows at each mini timestep multiplied by the mini timestep length, with the cumulatives reset to zero after each restart is written.
