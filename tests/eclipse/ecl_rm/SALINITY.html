This keyword can be used to define the salinity used in the modified Peng-Robinson equation of state in GASWAT cases and in the default CO2 solubility data in CO2SOL cases.

The units are molality (gm-M/kg).

UNITS: 10-3 kg-M/kg (METRIC), 10-3 lb-M/lb (FIELD), 10-3 gm-M/gm (LAB), 10-3 kg-M/kg (PVT-M)

In CO2SOL cases, this keyword corrects the solubility table and does not have any impact on the surface water density. DENSITY or GRAVITY can be used to that effect.

The relationship to the alternative definition in terms of parts per million by mass is described in "GASWAT option" in the ECLIPSE Technical Description.
