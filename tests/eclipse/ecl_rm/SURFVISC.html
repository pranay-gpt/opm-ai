The data comprises NTPVT (set in the TABDIMS keyword) tables of surfactant viscosity functions, each terminated by a slash (/), describing the effect on the viscosity of pure water of increasing the concentration of surfactant in solution. Each table consists of 2 columns of data:

  1. The surfactant concentration in the solution.

    The values should increase monotonically down the column.

    UNITS: kg/sm3 (METRIC), lb/stb (FIELD), gm/scc (LAB)

  2. The solution water viscosity at this surfactant concentration and the reference pressure (entered with the PVTW keyword).

    UNITS: cP (METRIC), cP (FIELD), cP (LAB), cP (PVT-M)

    If the Polymer Flood Model is active, then the viscosity input in the SURFVISC keyword will be multiplied by the polymer viscosity multiplier supplied in the PLYVISC or PLYVISCS keywords.

Note: The viscosity entered in this keyword at zero surfactant concentration should be equal to the reference viscosity entered in keyword PVTW (or PVTWSALT, whichever is used). This is required because the pure water viscosity update takes the form of a multiplier that is the ratio of reference viscosity divided by solution water viscosity, this ratio interpolated at the given surfactant concentration. Hence, at zero surfactant concentration, there will be no change to the water viscosity because the ratio modifier is equal to one.

Each table must contain at least two, and no more than NPPVT (set in the TABDIMS keyword) rows. Each row must be complete, containing an entry in every column of the table.

See "Surfactant model" in the ECLIPSE Technical Description for further information.

With NTPVT = 1 and NPPVT ≥ 3

 .01  0.38
