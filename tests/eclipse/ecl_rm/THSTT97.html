This keyword can only be used with the THERMAL option (keyword THERMAL in the RUNSPEC section).

If the keyword THSTT97 is used, the properties of water and steam will be based on an analytic formulation, as detailed by The International Association for the Properties of Water and Steam in the IAPWS Industrial formulation 1997 (IAPWS-IF97). The water and steam densities, the saturation pressures and temperatures, and the water and steam energies and enthalpies will be calculated using the IAPWS-IF97 formulation.
