The data specified consists of NTPVT (set in the TABDIMS keyword) tables of water pressure data, each terminated with a slash (/).

Each row of the table consists of 3 columns of data, and at least two rows of data should be entered.

Outside the range of specified pressures, values are linearly extrapolated.

  1. The water phase pressure.

    Values should increase down the column.

    UNITS: barsa (METRIC), psia (FIELD), atma (LAB), atma (PVT-M)

  2. The water formation volume factor

    Values should decrease down the column.

    UNITS: rm3/sm3 (METRIC), rb/stb (FIELD), rcc/scc (LAB), rm3/sm3 (PVT-M)

  3. The water viscosity.

    Values should be level or increasing down the column.

    UNITS: cP (METRIC), cP (FIELD), cP (LAB), cP (PVT-M)

Each table must contain no more than NPPVT (set in the TABDIMS keyword) rows. Each row must be complete, containing an entry in every column of the table.

Apart from the last or first rows, values in the second and third columns may be defaulted, and such values are linearly interpolated.
