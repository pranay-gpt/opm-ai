Examples

Example 1

With NTSFUN=1 and NSSFUN ≥ 5 and Metric units:

FOAMFST
--
--  Conc     ST
--  kg/sm3   N/m
---
     0.0     0.01
    10.0     0.005
    15.0     0.002
    20.0     0.001
    25.0     0.0001 /

Example 2

With NTSFUN=1 and NSSFUN ≥ 5 and Field units:

FOAMFST
--
--  Conc     ST
--  lb/stb   lbf/in
---
    0.0      0.000057
    3.5      0.000028
    5.25     0.000011
    7.0      0.0000057
    8.75     0.0000006 /

Example 3

With NTSFUN=1 and NSSFUN ≥ 5 and Lab units:

FOAMFST
--
--  Conc    ST
--  gm/scc  dyne/cm
---
    0.00    10.0
    0.01     5.0
    0.015    2.0
    0.02     1.0
    0.025    0.1 /

where the different sets of values are intended to be illustrative of the different orders of magnitudes associated with the different input unit systems.
