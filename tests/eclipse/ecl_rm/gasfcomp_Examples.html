Examples

Example 1

To place a single-level compressor in the pipeline from PLAT-A (simple compressor option):

GASFCOMP
-- GROUP  NEW VFP   NEW   GAS      COMP   SEQUENCE
-- NAME   TAB NO    ALQ   CONSUMP  LEVEL   NUMBER
 PLAT-A    1*      50    1000   /
/

Example 2

To declare a pair of two-level compressors in the pipelines from PLAT-A and PLAT-B, and have them turned on in sequence (extended compressor option):

GASFCOMP
-- GROUP  NEW VFP   NEW   GAS      COMP   SEQUENCE
-- NAME   TAB NO    ALQ   CONSUMP  LEVEL   NUMBER
  PLAT-A    1*       50   1000     1         1     /
  PLAT-A    1*      100   1500     2         2     /
  PLAT-B    1*       50   1000     1         3     /
  PLAT-B    1*      100   1500     2         4     /
/
