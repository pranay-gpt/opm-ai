Diffusivity multipliers

If the DIFFUSE option has been used in the RUNSPEC section to enable molecular diffusion to be modeled, diffusivities are calculated that reflect the effect of grid block properties upon diffusive flow. The calculation of these is described in "Diffusion" in the ECLIPSE Technical Description.

It is possible to set multipliers to act in these calculations, analogous to MULTX and similar keywords, in the normal convective flow case. The required keywords are DIFFMX, DIFFMY and DIFFMZ (or, for radial runs, DIFFMR, DIFFMTHT and DIFFMZ).

As in the case of transmissibility multipliers, the diffusivity multiplier acts between the cell and its neighbor in the positive direction. Refer also to DIFFMX-, DIFFMY- and DIFFMZ- (or, for radial runs, DIFFMR-, DIFFMTH- and DIFFMZ-) keywords.
