Examples

The examples show two ways to set items 21 and 34. The first example sets them on one line by defaulting the first 20 items, setting 21, defaulting the next 12 items and setting 34:

OPTIONS3
20* 2 12* 1 /

The second example sets the switches on two lines, by defaulting 20 items and setting item 21, then defaulting 33 items and setting item 34:

OPTIONS3
20* 2 /
OPTIONS3
33* 1 /
