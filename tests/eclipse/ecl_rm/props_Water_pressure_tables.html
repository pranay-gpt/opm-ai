Water pressure tables

The water compressibility and viscosity may be specified with the PVTW keyword. This might take the form:

PVTW
--Pref Bref   Cw        Vw    (dVw/dp)/Vw
  4000 1.001  0.000003  0.52  0      /

This specifies a reference pressure, the water formation volume factor at this pressure, the compressibility from this pressure and the water viscosity. The last value is a viscosibility, similar to compressibility, but for viscosity. In many cases this is zero. A quadratic function is used for extrapolation.

ECLIPSE 300

As an alternative to PVTW, the WATERTAB keyword may be used to enter a general variation of water properties with pressure.

When using the Brine option, the water PVT properties must be entered with the PVTWSALT keyword.
