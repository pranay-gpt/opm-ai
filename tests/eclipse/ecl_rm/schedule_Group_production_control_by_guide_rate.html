Keyword Description

WGRUPCON Sets or scales well guide rates for group control.
GUIDERAT Coefficients for a general formula for variable well guide rates.
GTADD Adds to a group rate target or limit by a given factor.
GTMULT Multiplies a group rate target or limit by a given factor.
