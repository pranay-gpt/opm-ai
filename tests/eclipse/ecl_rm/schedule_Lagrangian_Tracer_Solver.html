Lagrangian Tracer Solver (ECLIPSE 300)

ECLIPSE 300 keyword Description
LAGTRUPD Defines the streamline update conditions and solver method for the Lagrangian tracer solver.
LTRACE Forces a streamline trace at the end of the next timestep.
NOLTRACE Prevents streamline tracing until the next LTRACE or LAGTRUPD.
RPTSLN Controls the output of streamline data as SLN files.
