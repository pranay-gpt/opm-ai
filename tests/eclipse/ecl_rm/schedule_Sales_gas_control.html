Sales gas control (ECLIPSE 100)

This facility provides a means of controlling the export of associated gas from an oil field, which may at the same time have controls on the oil production rate. The gas export rate is controlled by reinjecting the surplus gas not required for sale.

  • GCONSALE Sales gas production targets and limits for groups and the field.
  • GCONSUMP Gas consumption and import rates.
  • WGRUPCON Well guide rates for group control.
  • WGASPROD Defines wells as special gas producers for sales gas control.
