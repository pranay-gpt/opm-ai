Scale deposition model

Keyword Description
SCDATAB Defines scale damage tables which govern the reduction in the connection transmissibility factor caused by deposited scale.
SCDPTAB Defines scale deposition tables which govern the rate of scale deposition (per unit water flow) at a well connection. This rate depends on the fraction of sea water present in the water flowing through the connection.
SCDPTRAC Sets a passive water tracer to be associated with the sea water fraction.
WSCTAB Specifies which scale deposition table and which scale damage table will be used to model scale effects for a well.
WSCCLEAN Reduces the amount of scale deposited around well connections
WSCCLENL Reduces the amount of scale deposited around local grid well connections
