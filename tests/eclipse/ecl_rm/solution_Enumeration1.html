Enumeration

You may specify the initial solution for every grid block using the following keywords

Note: The Enumeration option is not recommended for use in general.

Common keywords

Keyword Description
PRESSURE Cell pressures
SWAT Water saturations
SGAS Gas saturations
RS Liquid gas-oil ratios
RV Vapor oil-gas ratios
PBUB Initial bubble point pressures
PDEW Initial dew point pressures

ECLIPSE 100 keywords

  • PRVD Pressure versus depth variation.

ECLIPSE 300 keywords

  • SOIL Oil saturations
In the compositional case, all of PRESSURE, SWAT, SGAS, SOIL may be used, and the initial composition is defined by the following keywords.
  • XMF Liquid phase compositions
  • YMF Vapor phase compositions

The XMFVP and YMFVP keywords, entered in the PROPS section, may be used as an alternative to XMF and YMF to define initial phase compositions.

In the thermal case the temperature must be specified.
  • TEMPI Temperature

The TEMPVD keyword entered in the PROPS section may be used as an alternative to TEMPI.

As an alternative to entering these for the whole reservoir, the ADD, COPY, EQUALS and MULTIPLY keywords may be used to define values for PRESSURE, SWAT, SGAS, RS, RV.

See also the GETSOL and GETDATA keywords.
