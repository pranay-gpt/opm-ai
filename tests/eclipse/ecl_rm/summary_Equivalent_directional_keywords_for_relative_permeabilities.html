Equivalent directional keywords for relative permeabilities (ECLIPSE 100)

If any of the following options are active:

  • Directional Kr ('DIRECT' in RUNSPEC keyword SATOPTS)
  • Directional End-Point Scaling ('DIRECT' in RUNSPEC keyword ENDSCALE)
  • Vertical Equilibrium (RUNSPEC keyword VE)
  • Two-point Upstreaming for miscible option ('TWOPOINT' in RUNSPEC keyword MISCIBLE),

the following keywords may be used:
