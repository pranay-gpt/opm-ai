Initialization of quantities

A quantity is initialized using the first ASSIGN or DEFINE record referencing the quantity in the UDQ data. Upon initialization the elements of the quantity are undefined. When they are output or used with in an ACTION ( ACTIONG, ACTIONR, ACTIONW, ACTIONS, ACTIONX) keyword, undefined elements are replaced by the value specified in item 3 of UDQPARAM keyword.

Note: UDQs are only created for entities that exist at the time of initialization, so, for instance, defaulting the well name in a UDQ ASSIGN does not mean that all wells that will ever be defined will have a UDQ with that value. Instead, it means that the wells that are already defined when the UDQ is initialized will have that value. Wells that are defined subsequently will have an undefined element value. Similar logic applies to groups, connections, and segments.
This means that to initialize a UDQ for a particular entity (well, connection, etc) the UDQ keyword must come after the entity has been defined. In most cases this will be after one of the following keywords:
Well UDQs WELSPECS, WELSPECL
Connection UDQs COMPDAT, COMPDATL
Segment UDQs WELSEGS
Group UDQs WELSPECS, WELSPECL (with at least one well in the required group)
