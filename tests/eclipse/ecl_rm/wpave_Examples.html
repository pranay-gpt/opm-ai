Examples

Example 1

To give a connection factor weighted average, with increased weighting to the outer blocks:

WPAVE
 0.2 1.0 /

Example 2

To give a purely pore volume weighted average over all the blocks containing open or closed well connections and their neighbors:

WPAVE
 1* 0.0 1* ALL /

Example 3

To give a connection factor weighted average, where the pressure for each connection is the pore volume weighted average of pressures in the connection block and its outer ring of neighbors. The grid block pressures are not depth corrected:

WPAVE
 -1.0 1.0 NONE /
