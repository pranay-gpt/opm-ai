CO2STORE

The mutual solubilities of CO2 and H2O are calculated to match experimental data for typical CO2 storage conditions: typically 12-250 degrees C and up to 600 bars. The lower limit of 12 °C can be extended with the low temperature and/or hydrate formation options, discussed below. This extension is only applicable to systems with these two components and does not apply to systems with additional hydrocarbon. Solubilities are calculated following the procedure given by Spycher and Pruess (2005) and Spycher and Pruess (2009) , based on fugacity equilibration between water and a CO2 phase. Water fugacity is obtained by Henry's law, while CO2 fugacity is calculated using a modified Redlich-Kwong equation of state.

See "CO2 storage".

  • Salt precipitation is enabled by the use of the SOLID option. If the SOLID option is not used, salts are assumed to stay in the liquid phase.
  • The gas density is obtained by an accurately tuned cubic equation of state.
  • The brine density is first approximated by the pure water density and then corrected for salt and CO2 effects by Ezrokhi's method (Zaytsev and Aseyev, 1993) .
  • The CO2 gas viscosity is calculated from Vesovic et al (1990) and Fenghour et al (1998) .
