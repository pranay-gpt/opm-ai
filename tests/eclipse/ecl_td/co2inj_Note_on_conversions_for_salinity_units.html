Note on conversions for salinity units

As the various CO2 options use different frameworks within ECLIPSE, units for salinity input differ.

Salt concentrations in XMF and YMF must be entered in mole fraction. The SALINITY keyword unit is molality (gm-M/kg of solvent). Salinity in SOLUBILS must be expressed in kg/m3 of brine.

Conversions between these different units are described below.

In most of the cases, the solvent is H2O. As M H2O =18.014 gm/gm-M, molality H2O = 55.5 gm-M/kg.

Concentration (mg/L) to mole fraction

Chemical analysis usually gives concentration in mg/L (=10–3kg/m3) and the initial composition of the brine/water in ECLIPSE must be entered in mole fraction.

Molality to mass per volume unit (kg/m3)

Mass per volume unit = dB ×molality×10-3×M1+molality×10 -3 ×M

where

M = salt molecular weight (typically 58.44 kg/kg-M for NaCl)
B = brine volume factor
d = surface brine density
