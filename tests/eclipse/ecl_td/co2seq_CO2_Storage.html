CO2 storage

ECLIPSE 100
x ECLIPSE 300

In ECLIPSE 300 there are several options available in order to study the storage of CO2 under various conditions. This chapter describes the CO2STORE option, for CO2 - H2O systems with salts. In addition the following options are available.

  • The CO2SOL option, typically used to study CO2 storage in oil reservoirs in isothermal mode - water is not allowed in the oil/gas phase.
  • The GASWAT option, general equation of state for gas-water systems in isothermal mode.
  • The COAL option for coal bed methane reservoirs.

Since the CO2STORE, CO2SOL and the GASWAT options estimate the fluid properties differently, they cannot be used together.

In thermal mode the CO2STORE option can be used with any number of components and can include a liquid oil phase as described in Thermal CO2STORE.

Thus, in isothermal mode, CO2STORE can be used for CO2 storage simulations in saline aquifers, GASWAT can be used for CO2 storage simulations in depleted gas reservoirs, and CO2SOL can be used for CO2 storage simulations in depleted oil reservoirs. However, in thermal mode, CO2STORE thermal can be used to model CO2 storage simulations in saline aquifer and depleted oil and gas reservoirs. GASWAT and CO2SOL options are not compatible with thermal mode.
