Gas phase density

The gas formation volume factor B g is given by

Bg =(F⋅ Rv⋅Vg as, roil +V gas,  rgas)/ Vg as,s [Eq. 373.1]

where Vgas, r c is the reservoir gas phase volumes per mole of component c:

Vgas , rc= Zc ⋅R⋅T P [Eq. 373.2]

where

R is the gas constant,
P is the pressure,
T the temperature and the compressibility terms Z c can be set for each component using the ZFACTOR keyword to match equation 373.1 at the reservoir temperature.

The temperature dependence of these terms are fixed. A surface compressibility can be set using the ZFACTORS keyword to ensure the correct surface gas density:

Vga s,s =Z gas ⋅R⋅T P= MWgas ρg as,s [Eq. 373.3]
