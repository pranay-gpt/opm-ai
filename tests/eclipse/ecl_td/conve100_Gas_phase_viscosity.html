Gas phase viscosity

The thermal gas phase viscosity is given by equation 333.9 as a function of temperature and mole fractions. It can be rewritten in terms of R v to give:

μgas =1 F⋅R v+1⋅(F⋅R v⋅ μga soil+ μgasg as) [Eq. 375.1]

Values of the component gas phase viscosities μgas c can be found at the reservoir temperature to give a good fit for equation 375.1. These values can be entered in the GASVISCT keyword at two temperatures to give a temperature independent gas viscosity.
