K-values for live components

K-values can be calculated from R s and Rv values as functions of pressure:

Koil= Rs +F1 +F⋅R v ⋅Rv

Kgas= Rs +F1 +F⋅R v ⋅1 Rs

where

F is the ratio of the molar volumes of gas to oil at surface conditions:

F=Vgas,s V oil,s= MW gas MW oil ⋅ρ oil ,s ρg as,s

Values of ρoil ,s and ρ gas ,s can be taken from the DENSITY keyword. The units of F should be the same as those used for Rs and 1/Rv . In field units, a factor of 0.005615 can be used to convert ft3/ ft3 to M scf/ stb.

A K-value table (keyword KVTABTn) at reservoir temperature can be constructed from the R s and Rv data taken from the PVTO and PVTG keywords. At least one more K-value table is required at a different temperature. If no other data is available equation 330.2 can be used:
