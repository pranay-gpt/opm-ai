Oil phase viscosity

The thermal oil phase viscosity is given by equation 333.7 as a function of temperature and mole fractions. It can be rewritten in terms of R s to give:

log⁡μ oil =1F+R s⋅ (F⋅log ⁡μ oil oil +R s⋅log⁡μ oil gas ) [Eq. 374.1]

If necessary an extra pressure dependent factor can included in this formula. Values of the component oil phase viscosities μoil c can be found at the reservoir temperature to give a good fit for equation 374.1. These values can be entered in the OILVISCT keyword at two temperatures to give a temperature independent oil viscosity.
