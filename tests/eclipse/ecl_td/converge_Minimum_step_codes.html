Minimum step codes

Minimum step codes may start with the two letters Mn followed by an abbreviated version of one of the above - for example MnTP - where the second part indicates what constraint would have applied if the minimum step had not been imposed.

MnNC : Minimum timestep, No Convergence
MnSC : Minimum timestep due to Solution change
MnTP : Minimum timestep due to Throughput change
MnPC : Minimum timestep due to Pressure change
MnTT : Minimum timestep due to Time Truncation error
MnTE : Minimum timestep due to Temperature change (thermal)
MnEn : Minimum timestep due to Energy change (thermal)
MnSA : Minimum timestep due to Saturation change (thermal)
