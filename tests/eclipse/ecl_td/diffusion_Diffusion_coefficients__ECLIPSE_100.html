Diffusion coefficients: ECLIPSE 100

ECLIPSE 100 allows diffusion of both gas and oil components within both the oil and gas phases. For example, in a gas condensate model with no oil phase present, gas diffuses from a low R v region to a high R v region, and vaporized oil flows in the opposite direction from the high Rv to the low R v region ( Rv is the vaporized oil-gas ratio). Consider a gas component in the gas phase. The moles of gas and oil within the gas phase are given by:

Mgas =Sg⋅ ρg B g⋅ MWg [Eq. 713.1]
Moi l= Rv ⋅Sg⋅ρ o Bg ⋅MW o [Eq. 713.2]

hence the gas total molar concentration c is

cg=S g Bg ( ρg MW g+ Rv⋅ρ o MWo )=S g⋅ ρg Bg⋅MWg (1+ Rv F) [Eq. 713.3]

The gas mole fraction is

x g=ρg/MW gρg/MWg+Rvρo /MWo =1 1+RvF [Eq. 713.4]

with

F =ρ o⋅ MWg ρg⋅MWo [Eq. 713.5]

where

S g is the gas saturation
B g is the gas formation volume factor
ρ g is the surface density of gas
ρ o is the surface density of oil
MW g is the molecular weight of gas
MW o is the molecular weight of oil
R v is the vaporized oil-gas ratio

The diffusive flow of gas at surface conditions F gg is given by:

Fgg =MW g⋅ Jg ⋅A⋅φ ρg [Eq. 713.6]

For more information see Reid et al (1987) . The gas-in-gas diffusion coefficient D gg is given by:

Jg=-c g⋅ Dgg ⋅dxg dl [Eq. 713.7]

with

d xg= -  F(1+Rv F)2 dRv

Substituting gives the diffusive flow of gas:

Fgg =S g Bg ⋅F 1+ RvF⋅ Dgg ⋅d Rv dl  ⋅A⋅ φ [Eq. 713.8]

where

J g is the molar flux of gas per unit area
dxg dl is the mole fraction gradient of gas
A is the area of flow
φ is the porosity

In the simulator the diffusive flow of gas from cell i to j is:

Fgg =S g Bg ⋅F (1+ Rv F)×Dg g×(Rv j- Rvi )d ×A×φ

or

Fgg =-  SgBg ⋅F 1+ RvF⋅ Dgg ⋅(Rvi ⁢-  Rvj )⋅ Td [Eq. 713.9]

where

T d is the diffusivity = A φ/d
d is the distance between cell centers
R vi is the vapor oil-gas ratio for cell i
R vj is the vapor oil-gas ratio for cell j
S g is taken as M IN(Sgi , S gj)
B g is taken as ( Bgi +Bgj )/2
R v is taken as ( Rvi +Rvj )/2

For the oil-in-gas diffusive flow relationship F og:

Fog =MW o⋅ Jo ⋅A⋅φ ρo

and the oil-in-gas diffusion coefficient D og is given by:

Jo=-c g⋅ Dog ⋅dxo dl

cg=S g Bg ⋅ ρg MW g( 1+R vF )

x o= RvF 1+RvF

d xo= F (1+R vF) 2dRv

Fog =-  SgBg ⋅1 (1+ Rv F)×Do g×(Rv j - R vi)d× A×φ

Fog =S g Bg ×1 1+ RvF× Dog ×(Rvi -R vj) ×T d

where

J o is the molar flux of oil per unit area
dxo dl is the mole fraction gradient of oil

For the oil-in-oil diffusive flow relationship F oo:

Foo =MW o⋅ Jo ⋅A⋅φ ρo

and the oil-in-oil diffusion coefficient D oo is given by:

Jo=-c⋅ Doo⋅ dxo dl

c =S o Bo ⋅ ρo MW o( Rs+F F)

x o=F F+R s

d xo= -  F(F+Rs )2dRs

Foo =S o Bo ⋅1 (F+ Rs )×Doo ×(Rsj  -  Rsi )d ×A×φ

Foo =-  SoBo ×1 (F+ Rs )×Doo ×(R si -Rsj) ×T d

For the gas-in-oil diffusive flow relationship F go:

Fgo =MW g⋅ Jg ⋅A⋅φ ρg

and the gas-in-oil diffusion coefficient D go is given by:

Jg=-c o⋅ Dgo ⋅dxg dl

co=S o Bo ⋅ ρo MW o( F+Rs F)

x g=RsF+Rs

d xg= F (F+Rs)2 dRs

Fgo =-  SoBo ⋅F (F+ Rs )×Dgo ×(Rsj  -  Rsi )d ×A×φ

Fgo =S o Bo ×F (F+ Rs )×Dgo ×(R si -Rsj) ×T d
