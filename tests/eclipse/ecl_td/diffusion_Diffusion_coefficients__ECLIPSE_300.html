Therefore, there are two possible ways of specifying diffusion coefficients:

  • Use normal diffusion coefficients D i defined by keywords DIFFCOIL (DIFFCWAT for GASWAT or CO2STORE option) and DIFFCGAS.
  • Use activity corrected diffusion coefficients Dia defined by keywords DIFFAOIL and DIFFAGAS. Thermal diffusion coefficients Di T can optionally be defined by keywords DIFFTOIL and DIFFTGAS.
