Radial diffusivity calculations

In this case, the expressions used are based upon true radial diffusive flow between the pressure equivalent radii, with a dip correction.

Radial diffusivity

The radial diffusivity is:

TDrI = MDrIFdip1TI +1 TJ [Eq. 724.1]

with:

TI= ϕIR ntgI Dθ IDzI D1p [Eq. 724.2]
D1p = R12( R2 2⁢−  R 12)ln⁡ (R 1/R2) +1 2 [Eq. 724.3]

and

TJ= ϕJR ntgJ Dθ JDzJ D2m [Eq. 724.4]
D2m = R3 2( R3 2- R 22 )ln (R 3 R2 )-12 [Eq. 724.5]

where R1 is the inner radius of cell I, R2 the outer radius of cell I, and R 3 the outer radius of cell J.

Azimuthal diffusivities

The theta-direction diffusivity is given by:

T DθI = MDθ IFdip1TI +1 TJ

with

T I=2ϕIRntg ID zIln (R2 R1 ) DθI

where R1 is the inner radius, and R2 is the outer radius. The expression for Tj is defined similarly.

ln() implies the natural logarithm throughout. This expression is also used in completing the circle in radial geometry.

Vertical diffusivities

The Z-direction diffusivity is given by:

T DzI =M DzI 1 TI +1 TJ

with

TI =ϕ IDθ I( R2 2-R1 2)D zI

where R1 is the inner radius, and R2 is the outer radius. The expression for Tj is defined similarly.
