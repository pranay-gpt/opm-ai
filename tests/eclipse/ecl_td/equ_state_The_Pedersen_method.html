The Pedersen method

If the Pedersen et alviscosity calculation ( Pedersen et al, 1989 ) is required instead of the default method, then the PEDERSEN keyword should be added to the PROPS section. The calculation is based on a modification of the corresponding states principle in which the mixture viscosity μ is related to a reference viscosity μ o by terms involving properties of the mixture (denoted with a subscript m) and the reference material (denoted with a subscript o). The properties are the critical temperature T c, critical pressure P c, molecular weight M w and rotational coupling factor α:

μ(P,T)=( Tc mTco )- 1/6 ⋅( Pcm P co )2/ 3⋅(M wm Mwo) 1/2 ⋅(αm αo )⋅ μo (P o, To)

The reference material is taken to be methane. Pedersen ( Pedersen et al, 1989 ) gives formulas for the mixture properties, and the reference pressure P o and reference temperature T o. The mixture molecular weight is given by:

Mwm =c 1⋅1.304 ×10−4⋅(M ¯ w c2 ⋅2.303− M ¯n c 2⋅2.303)+ M ¯n

where M ¯w and M ¯ n are the weight average and number average mole weights. The rotational coupling factors are given in terms of the reduced density ρ rm by:

αm=1+c 5⋅ ρr mc3 ⋅M wm c4

αo=1+c 6⋅ ρr mc3

The coefficients c 1, ..., c6 can be defined by the PEDTUNE and PEDTUNER keywords. Note that the default value of c 6 is related to the default values of c 4 and c5 by

c6=c5 ⋅Mwo c4
