This modified equation is intended to improve the modeling of the behavior of heavy oils. It was requested by a client and has not been published.

In this equation the viscosity increases exponentially as the reduced molar density br approaches a maximum bmax ,r. For small values of br, such that br«bmax ,r, the modified Lorentz-Bray-Clark equation has the same form as the original equation, Equation 224.1. When entering Vmin,i data it is important to ensure that the actual molar density never exceeds the maximum, so that
