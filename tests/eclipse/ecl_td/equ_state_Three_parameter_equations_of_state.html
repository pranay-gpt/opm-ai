Three parameter equations of state

The traditional weakness of the two-parameter equations of state (EoS), such as the Peng-Robinson, Redlich-Kwong for example, is their poor prediction of liquid properties, especially liquid densities and saturations.

The critical compressibilities predicted by the Van der Waals, Redlich-Kwong and Peng-Robinson equations of state are Z cVdW =0.375, Z cRK =0.333 and Z cpr =0.307. But hydrocarbons are known to have Z c<0.29.

Peneloux et al. proposed a molar volume correction, referred to as volume shift, which adds a third parameter to the EoS, and, in turn, greatly improves liquid property estimation.
