Aquifers

The temperature of the aquifer water or gas is specified in the aquifer definition keyword (see the AQUCT, AQUFETP, AQUCHWAT, AQUCHGAS or AQUFLUX keywords) for analytic aquifers. If the temperature of the analytic aquifer is defaulted and the TEMPVD keyword is specified, the aquifer temperature will be interpolated from the TEMPVD table using the aquifer depth. Otherwise if the temperature of the analytic aquifer is defaulted and the TEMPVD keyword is not specified, the RTEMP temperature will be used.

The temperature of the numerical aquifer water is constant. If the TEMPVD keyword is used, the aquifer temperature will be the temperature at the aquifer depth. Otherwise the RTEMP temperature is used.
