Steam trap

In DEADOIL simulations, steam production can be prevented by using either the WELLPROD or WCONPROD keyword to set the well BHP above the saturated water pressure in the well cells. The difference between the BHP and the saturated water pressure can be controlled either by specifying a pressure offset or a temperature offset.

The same keywords can be used to control the BHP in LIVEOIL simulations. However, if the reservoir contains volatile hydrocarbons, some steam may be produced at pressures above the water saturation pressure.
