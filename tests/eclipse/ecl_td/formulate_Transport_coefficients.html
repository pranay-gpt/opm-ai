Transport coefficients

The component mobilities can be modified in ECLIPSE 300 by including transport coefficients ( Barker and Fayers, 1994 ). The transport coefficients αpc act as multipliers to the mobility. Thus equation 667.2 becomes:

Mp c= αpcx pc kr p bpmμp [Eq. 668.1]

where

α pc= αpc(zt) is the transport coefficient given by the ALPHA keyword
z t is the mole fraction of component t defined by the TRCOEF keyword.
