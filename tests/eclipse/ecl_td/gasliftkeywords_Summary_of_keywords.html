Summary of keywords

SUMMARY

Refer to Well and group gas flows in the ECLIPSE Reference Manual.

Note: In the presence of gas lift optimization, the produced gas reported (for example using the FGPR, GGPR and WGPR summary section keywords) does not include produced lift gas, only produced formation gas.

SCHEDULE

Restriction Keyword Description
  GLIFTOPT Sets limits on group lift gas supply.
  LIFTOPT Activates gas lift optimization and sets some governing parameters.
ECLIPSE 100 VFPPROD Inputs VFP tables.
ECLIPSE 100 VFPTABL Selects the method of interpolating the ALQ variable in VFP tables.
  WLIFTOPT Sets gas lift optimization data for wells.
