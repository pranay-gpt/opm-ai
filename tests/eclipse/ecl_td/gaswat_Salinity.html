Salinity

In the above, salinity is defined by molality: cs =1000⋅ns/ mw , where ns is the number of moles of salt, for instance in kg-M, and mw the mass of the water, for instance in kg. This may be related to the usual definition in terms of parts per million by mass:

csppm⁢ = A⋅ cs ⁢/ ( 1⁢+ B⋅c s) [Eq. 216.1]
cs=cs ppm /(A-B⋅c sppm) [Eq. 216.2]

with A = 103MNaCl, B = 10-3MNaCl, MNaCl = 58.44 kg/kg-M

The salinity variation of the equation of state coefficients is not treated on a cell by cell basis - a single value is entered using the SALINITY keyword in the PROPS section. If it is not entered the salinity value is assumed to be zero.
