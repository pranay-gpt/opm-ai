The conventional Peng-Robinson equation of state

The A -coefficient in the Peng-Robinson equation of state is given by:

A =Σjk xjx kA jk [Eq. 214.1]

where x are component mole fractions and

Ajk =(1 -k jk) (Aj⁢ Ak )12 [Eq. 214.2]

kjk are binary interaction coefficients. A jk is defined as:

Aj=Ω jP rj Tr j2 [Eq. 214.3]

The reduced critical temperatures and pressures are T r=T/ Tc and P r=P/ Pc. The reduced A-coefficients are defined by extracting this temperature and pressure dependence:

Aj=A jr ⋅P T2 [Eq. 214.4]

The Peng-Robinson equation of state then defines Ajr as:

Ajr= Ωoj T cj2 αj P cj [Eq. 214.5]

with

αj 1 2= 1+f( wj )(1 -Trj1 2 ) [Eq. 214.6]

f(w j) is a polynomial in the acentric factor, given by:

f (w j)= 0.37464+1.54226 wj -0.26996wj2 [Eq. 214.7]

Rewriting to account for the temperature dependence in this expression gives:

Aj r= ΩojTcj 2( pj +q jT 12) 2 Pc j [Eq. 214.8]

where pj=1+f( wj ) and qj=-f( wj )⁢/ Tc1 2 .

Then:

Arjk =[1-k jk][ Ωo jT cj2Pcj ] 12 [ Ωo kT ck2Pck ] 12 [pj pk +( pj qk +p kqj )T 12 + qj qk T] [Eq. 214.9]

A1jk =(1-k jk)( Ωo jT cj2Pcj ) 12 ( Ωo kT ck2Pck ) 12 (p jpk) [Eq. 214.10]
A2jk =(1-k jk)( Ωo jT cj2Pcj ) 12 ( Ωo kT ck2Pck ) 12 (pj qk +p kq j) [Eq. 214.11]
A3jk =(1-k jk)( Ωo jT cj2Pcj ) 12 ( Ωo kT ck2Pck ) 12 [q jqk] [Eq. 214.12]
