Economic limits

The keyword GCALECON allows minimum economic limits to be applied to a group’s energy rate and to its mean calorific value. If a group violates either of these limits its subordinate producers will be closed, and optionally the run may be ended.

GCALECON
-- Group       Min Economic     Min Economic      End run flag
-- name        energy rate     calorific value
  FIELD           1.0E9 /
  ‘G*’            5.0E7            20000.0             YES  /
/
