Energy injection

ECLIPSE does not allow direct control over injected energy rates or net (that is the produced minus injected) energy rates. However, energy sales rates can be reported in the SUMMARY file, which represent the energy produced minus energy injected minus the energy content of any consumed gas. Injected energy can be taken into account by giving the gas injection wells a specified calorific value with the WCALVAL keyword. Alternatively, if a tracer is used to describe the energy content of the gas, the WTRACER keyword can be used to set the tracer value in the injection wells. Gas injection should not be applied when using the Gas Field Operations Model.
