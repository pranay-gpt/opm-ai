Consistency requirements

Certain consistency relationships between the end-points on the imbibition and drainage curves should be met in order to use the above models. As discussed above, the imbibition and drainage curves should meet at the maximum saturation Snmax . Clearly also Sncri> Sncrd is required.

ECLIPSE 100

ECLIPSE 100 checks these consistency relationships if the HYSTCHCK keyword is included in the PROPS section.
