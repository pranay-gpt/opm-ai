Heavy oil

ECLIPSE 300 Thermal

If oil is heavier than water (based on the composition provided at the datum depth) and if the water-oil contact lies above the reservoir, then the reservoir will be initialized with heavy oil with the datum pressure being interpreted as the pressure of the oil phase. A non-zero value for item 217 in OPTIONS3 can be used to restore the pre-2010.1 behavior for thermal heavy oil equilibration.
