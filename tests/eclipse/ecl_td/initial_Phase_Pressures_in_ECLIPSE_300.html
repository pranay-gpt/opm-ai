Phase pressures in ECLIPSE 300

By default, oil and gas phase pressures are derived from the pressure solution variable P using the formula

Poil=P -  Pco g×( Sg So +Sg ) [Eq. 385.1]

and

P gas=P+Pco g×( So So+ Sg) [Eq. 385.2]

where

P cog is the gas-oil capillary pressure,
S o and Sg are the oil and gas saturations respectively.

This Pc splitting technique is performed to enable better convergence and property smoothness above the critical point. It may be disabled using keyword FORMOPTS, option NOPCSPT. When reporting initialization data using the RPTRST or RPTSOL keywords, the pressures are defined as

PRESSURE is the solution variable P which is derived from the phase pressures calculated during the EQUIL hydrostatic equilibration and reported with the SOLVD option in RPTSOL.
POIL is the oil phase pressure P oil defined in equation 385.1
PGAS is the gas phase pressure P gas defined in equation 385.2
Note: The Pc splitting technique applies only to the ECLIPSE 300 compositional mode.
