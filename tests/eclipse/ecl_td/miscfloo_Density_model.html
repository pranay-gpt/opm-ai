Density model

The treatment of effective oil and gas densities is based on the same 1/4-power rule as the effective viscosities. By default the density calculation will use the same mixing parameter as the viscosity. However, a separate mixing parameter may optionally be specified for the effective density calculation.

The densities are computed as follows. First, the partially mixed or effective viscosities are calculated using equations 396.1, 396.2 and 396.4. The value of each effective component viscosity is then used to yield an effective saturation fraction to be used in oil and gas density calculations:

(S o Sn )oe= μo 1/4 (μ o eff 1/4  -  μg1/4 )μ o eff 1/4 ⋅(μ o1/4  - μ g1/4) [Eq. 397.1]
(S o Sn )ge= μo 1/4 (μ g eff1 /4 - μg1/4 )μ g eff 1/4 ⋅(μ o1/4⁢-   μg1/4 ) [Eq. 397.2]

The effective oil and gas densities ( ρoeff  ,ρg eff) are now computed from the effective saturations fractions in expressions in equations 397.1 and 397.2 and the pure component densities (ρo  ,ρg) using the following formulae:

ρ o eff =ρo (S oSn) oe+ ρg[ 1-( So Sn )oe ] [Eq. 397.3]
ρ g eff =ρo (S oSn) ge+ ρg[ 1-( So Sn )ge ] [Eq. 397.4]

For the fully mixed case, that is ω=1, the effective saturations are equal:

(S o Sn )oe=( SoSn )ge = μo1/4( μm1/4 -μ g1/4) μ m1/4 ⋅( μo1/4-μg1/4 )

and the effective densities are therefore equal, ρ o eff =ρg eff=ρm.

For the unmixed, fully segregated case, that is ω=0, the effective saturations simplify to

(So Sn )oe =1, (So Sn )ge =0

and the effective densities are equal to the “pure” phase densities ρ o eff =ρo, ρ g eff =ρg.

The model allows you to specify a different surface density for the solvent gas to that for the solution gas. In equations 397.3 and 397.4, the pure solvent gas density at reservoir conditions, ρg , is computed from the solvent gas surface density input with the SDENSITY keyword. The oil density at reservoir conditions, ρo , is calculated from the stock tank oil and solution gas surface densities.

Note that equations 397.1 and 397.2 are singular for unit mobility ratio displacements. In that case, where μ o=μg, the effective densities are computed as follows. First a mixture density is defined as:

ρ m=ρ o(SoS n)+ρg(Sg Sn ) [Eq. 397.5]

Then:

ρ o eff =(1 -ω)ρo+ ωρm [Eq. 397.6]
ρ g eff =(1 -ω)ρg+ ωρm [Eq. 397.7]

where is ω the Todd and Longstaff parameter input with the TLMIXPAR keyword.
