Effect of water saturation

A feature of miscible gas injection processes that may also be modeled is the screening effect of high water saturation on the contact between the miscible gas and the oil in-place in each grid cell. The effective residual oil saturation to a miscible gas drive is found to increase with increasing water saturation and correct modeling of the effect is important since it may reduce the efficiency of the miscible displacement.
