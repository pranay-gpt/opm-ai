Assumptions

Gassmann fluid substitution equation

The Petro-elastic Model uses the Gassmann fluid substitution equation.

The use of this equation relies on the following assumptions:

  • The seismic frequencies used are sufficiently low (less than 100 Hz) such that the induced pore pressures are equilibrated throughout the pore space (that is there is sufficient time for the pore volume fluid to flow and eliminate wave-induced pore pressure gradients).
  • There is a homogeneous mineral modulus.
  • The rock is isotropic.
  • The fluid bearing rock is completely saturated.

Fluid viscosity

The fluids are assumed to be not "too" viscous so that the shear stiffness of the reservoir is entirely due to the shear stiffness of the dry rock frame.
