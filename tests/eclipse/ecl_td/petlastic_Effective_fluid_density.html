Effective fluid density

The effective fluid density is the saturation weighted average of the individual fluid component densities, that is,

ρ fluid=S oρo +Sw ρw +Sg ρg [Eq. 846.1]

where

S o/w/g is the saturation of oil/water/gas in the grid cell
ρ o/w/g is the density of oil/water/gas in the grid cell

These quantities are all available within the simulator for each grid cell.
