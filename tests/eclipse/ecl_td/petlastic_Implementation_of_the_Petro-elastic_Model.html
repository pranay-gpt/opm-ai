Implementation of the petro-elastic model

Inputs

The following values, which are not normally present in the simulation model, are required to specify the Petro-elastic Model:

These are specified as constants on each petro-elastic region

S the salinity of the water in equation 854.2
T the temperature in equation 853.2, equation 854.2, and equation 855.1 (this may be available elsewhere in the simulation model but the petro-elastic temperature is specified separately)
ρ minerals the effective density of the minerals in equation 843.3
C 1 the first order coefficient of effective porosity in the calculation of the effective bulk modulus of the minerals in equation 848.1
C 0 the zeroth order coefficient of effective porosity in the calculation of the effective bulk modulus of the minerals in equation 848.1
C K is the coefficient of the Kminerals term in the calculation of K frame in equation 850.1
C G the coefficient of the Gminerals term in the calculation of G frame in equation 851.1
G minerals the effective shear modulus of the minerals in equation 851.1
α the exponent in the calculation of the effective bulk/shear modulus of the dry rock frame in equation 850.1 and 851.1
e the "patchy saturation" exponent in equation 847.2 (only used if Wood's Law with Brie et al is requested). Switch to select between Generalized Wood's Law and Wood's Law with Brie et al in the calculation of the effective bulk modulus of the fluid
CK,i (P) the tabular functions of pressure in the calculation of the effective bulk modulus of the dry rock frame in equation 850.1
CG,i (P) the tabular functions of pressure in the calculation of the effective shear modulus of the dry rock frame in equation 851.1

Outputs

The output from the Petro-elastic Model depends on which simulated seismic-derived properties are requested (from the list of P-wave impedance, S-wave impedance and Poisson's ratio). When a simulated seismic-derived property is requested, it is output as a value on each active grid cell at each report time.

One use of these seismic-derived quantities is in history-matching using seismic data. In this application, gradients of simulated seismic-derived quantities with respect to uncertain reservoir model parameters may be required. In addition to simulated seismic-derived quantities, the Petro-elastic Model can calculate and output these gradients. For these calculations to be performed, you must specify a Petro-elastic Model (using the keywords described in "Summary of keywords") and request gradients. For further information on requesting gradients, see "Gradient Option".
