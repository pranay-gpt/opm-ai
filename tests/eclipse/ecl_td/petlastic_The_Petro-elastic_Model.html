The petro-elastic model

Most of the theory of this model (that is the assumptions and equations) can be found in Mavko et al (1998) .

The petro-elastic model can be decomposed into four levels. These are:

  • "Level 1: Impedances and velocities"
  • "Level 2: Effective bulk/shear modulus and density"
  • "Level 3: Rock properties and fluid properties"
  • "Level 4: Individual fluid phase properties"

Level 1 is the output and it requires results from the calculations in Level 2. In turn, Level 2 requires the results from the calculations in Level 3 and so on.
