Restrictions

The following features of the ECLIPSE 100 polymer flood model are currently not available in the ECLIPSE 300 polymer flood model:

  • The effect of Todd-Longstaff mixing parameter to model the physical dispersion at the leading edge of the slug and the fingering effects at the rear edge of the slug.
  • The effect of temperature variations on the behavior of the polymer solution.
  • The effect of reduced relative permeability of the polymer solution.
  • The effect of dead pore volume on increasing the velocity of the polymer relative to the water phase velocity.
  • The effect of non-Newtonian rheology.
