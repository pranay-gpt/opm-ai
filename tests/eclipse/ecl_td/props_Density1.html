Density

The oil density is defined as

ρ o=ρref ,o [1- cPo (P- Pref )] [1+c T1( T-Tref)+c T2(T-T ref )2] [Eq. 336.1]

where

the oil compressibility cPo and the thermal expansion coefficients c T1 and c T2 are defined by the OILCOMPR keyword,
ρ ref ,o is defined by the DENSITY keyword and
T ref and P ref are defined by the STCOND keyword.
