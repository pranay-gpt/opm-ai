Rock heat capacity

The enthalpy per unit volume of rock is given by

H r=C r0( T-Tref)+ Cr 1(T-Tref )2 2 [Eq. 322.1]

where

the volumetric heat capacity Cr0 is defined by the HEATCR keyword,
the temperature coefficient Cr1 is defined by the HEATCRT keyword
the reference temperature Tref is specified with the STCOND keyword.
