Steam viscosity

The steam viscosity is taken from Ejiogu and Fiori (1987) .

μ g=Ag +Bg TC+Cg PPD g [Eq. 345.1]

where

the temperature T C is in °C,

the pressure P P is in MPa

and the default coefficients are:

Ag =4.9402× 10-3, Bg=5.0956×10 -5,C g=2.9223×10- 6, Dg =2.5077

The coefficients maybe modified using the THSVC keyword.
