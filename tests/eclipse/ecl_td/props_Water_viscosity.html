Water viscosity

The water viscosity can be defined in tabular form (WATVISCT) or in functional form (default). The functional form is taken from Grabowski and Rubin (1981) .

μ w=Aw- 1+B wTF +Cw TF2 [Eq. 344.1]

where

the temperature T F is in °F and

the default coefficients are A w=2.1850 Bw=0.04012 Cw =5.1547× 10-6

The coefficients maybe modified using the THWVC keyword.

Water viscosity pressure dependence

The water viscosity can be multiplied by a factor μp ⁡(P) /μ p⁡ (Pvref ) where μ p and Pvref are defined by the keywords PVTW and VISCREF.
