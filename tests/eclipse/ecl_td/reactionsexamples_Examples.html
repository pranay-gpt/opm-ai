Example 2: A dynamic approach to equilibrium

In this example two components are used to model the oil phase and gas phase of a single hydrocarbon. The flash is replaced by an equilibrium-deviation reaction, representing evaporation and condensation.

The following keyword is needed in the RUNSPEC section:

REACTION
1 1 1 / one reaction, one deviation term, one EQLD k-value

In the PROPS section:

CNAMES
C_OIL C_GAS /

CVTYPE
DEAD  GAS / Switch off the flash for these components

STOREAC
