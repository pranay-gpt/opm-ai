Details of the recovery calculation

ECLIPSE solves the oil conservation equation:

d (OIP) =PV t+dt ⋅S ot+ dt⋅ [1 Bo ]t +dt -PV t⋅Sot ⋅[ 1Bo ]t [Eq. 652.1]

Some care is needed to associate the production mechanisms such that the total oil produced equals the change in oil-in-place, d (OIP). Below we show how the production associated with each recovery mechanism is calculated.

Let:

PV t+dt =PV t+d(PV) S ot+dt= So t+d(S o) [ 1B o] t+dt =[ 1B o ]t+d[1 Bo] d(OIP)= [PV+d (PV)] [S o+d (S o)] [1 Bo +d( 1Bo )]-[ PV⋅S o⋅ 1B o ] [Eq. 652.2]

Expanding the conservation equation,

d( OIP)=d (PV)⋅So t⋅ [1 Bo ] t+d (PV)⋅ So t⋅d[1 Bo ] +PV t⋅d (So)⋅ [1 Bo ]t +d( PV)⋅d (S o)⋅ [1 Bo ] t+PVt⋅So t⋅d[1 Bo ] +PV t⋅d (So)⋅d[1 Bo ]+d (PV)⋅d(So) ⋅d[ 1B o] [Eq. 652.3]

The terms in this equation that are associated with the various production mechanisms are:

Rock Compaction =DC=- d(PV) ⋅S ot ⋅[ 1B o ]t+d t Saturation Change=DS =-PV t+dt ⋅d(So )⋅ [1 Bo ]t Oil Expansion =DE=- PVt ⋅Sot +dt⋅d[1 Bo] -d(PV )⋅d( So )⋅d[1 Bo ] [Eq. 652.4]

Now, d (S o)= -d( Sw )-d( Sg ), giving the final breakdown of the production:

Rock Compaction =DC=- d(PV) ⋅Sot⋅[1 Bo] t+dt Water Influx =DW= PVt+dt⋅d (S w)⋅ [1 Bo ] tGas Influx=DG=PVt+dt ⋅d(Sg) ⋅[ 1B o] t Oil Expansion=DE=- PVt ⋅S ot+ dt⋅d[1 Bo ]-d(PV) ⋅d( So )⋅d[ 1Bo ] [Eq. 652.5]

If gas saturation is greater than zero then:
