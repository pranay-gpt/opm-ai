Required input data (E100)

To activate the net carbon production/injection tracking feature in ECLIPSE 100, the 'carbon content' must be specified for either oil or gas (or both) with the CARBOIL and CARBGAS keywords. The carbon content is defined as the weight-moles of carbon per surface volume in the unit system used in the model, and a value can be supplied for each PVT region. As for other per-PVT-region keywords, if a region other than the first is defaulted, it takes the value from the previous region. The PVT region used in all calculations is the region of the producing or injecting well.

Carbon content cannot be specified for water.
