Required input data (E300)

To activate the net carbon production/injection tracking feature in ECLIPSE 300, the 'carbon content' must be specified for any components that contain carbon with the CARBON keyword. The carbon content of a component is defined as the number of moles of carbon per mole of the component. For example, one mole of propane (C3H8) contains three moles of carbon atoms so it has a carbon content value of 3.

Carbon content cannot be specified for water components.

Note: In E300, CARBON can be used for Black oil simulation. Internally, the black oil description is converted to an OIL and a GAS component. The first value will correspond to the OIL component and the next one to GAS. Note that the molecular weight of OIL is hardcoded to 120 and the molecular weight of GAS is hardcoded to 25.
