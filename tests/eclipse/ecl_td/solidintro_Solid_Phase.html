Solid components can be used with the Black oil, Equation of State (EoS), K-value, or Thermal options. Within the reservoir, these components remain in the solid phase throughout the simulation. However, solid components can be converted to fluid components through a chemical reaction. A solid component could be used, for example, to simulate production and take up of coke during combustion.

Solids can be either suspended in the oil phase, or deposited on to the formation. Adsorption isotherms can be defined which determine the fraction of solids in suspension.

Suspended solids are transported with the flow.

Solids that are deposited on the formation reduce the pore volume available for flow. Permeability multipliers can be used to model this effect.
