Solid saturations

When solids are present the pore volume V p is taken up by both fluids and solids

Vp=Vf +V s= Vp⋅(V flu+Vs ol)

where the specific fluid volume is given by

V flu =Voi l+V gas +Vwa t

Normalized fluid saturations are defined as fractions of the fluid volume

S o=Voil Vf lu , S g=Vgas Vf lu , S w=Vwat Vf lu

The normalized saturations satisfy the equation:

S o+Sg +Sw =1

Saturations can also be defined as fractions of the pore volume:

S^ s= Vsol

S^ o= Voil= Vflu ⋅S o=( 1- S^ s) ⋅So

S^ g= Vgas= Vflu ⋅S g=( 1- S^ s) ⋅Sg

S^ w= Vwat= Vflu ⋅S w=( 1- S^ s) ⋅Sw

These modified saturations satisfy the equation:

S^ o+ S^g +S ^ w+ S^ s=1

The solid saturation S^ and the multiplier 1 -S^ are used to calculate permeability reduction and initial conditions.
