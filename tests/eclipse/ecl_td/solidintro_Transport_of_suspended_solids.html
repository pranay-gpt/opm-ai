Transport of suspended solids

Solids adsorbed to the rock are immobile, but the suspended fraction will be transported with the oil phase. It is assumed that the concentration of suspended solids is small, so that they can be treated as a tracer - that is the oil flow is not modified by the presence of suspended solids, and the mobility of a solid component c is given by
