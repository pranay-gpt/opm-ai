Density model

The treatment of effective oil, gas and solvent densities is based on the fourth power law for the effective viscosities above. By default, the density calculation can be based on the same mixing parameter as the viscosity, however a separate mixing parameter may be specified for the effective density calculation if required.

The densities are computed by the following procedure. First, the effective viscosities are calculated using equations 409.1 to 409.4 above. The value of each effective component viscosity is then used in turn in equations 409.2 to 409.4 to yield an effective saturation fraction to be used in oil, gas and solvent density calculations as:

(S o Sn )oe= μo  1/4(μo eff  1/ 4  −  μs  1/4 ) μo eff  1/ 4( μo  1/4 −   μ s 1/4 ) [Eq. 410.1]
(S o Sn )ge= μs  1/4( μg eff  1/ 4−  μ g 1/4 )μ g eff  1/4(μs 1/4 −  μg 1/4) [Eq. 410.2]
(S s Sn )se= μs  1/4( Sg f  μo  1/4+S of  μ g 1 /4) -μ o 1/4 μg  1/4( μs  1/4μs eff  1/ 4) μ s 1/4( S gf  μo 1/4+ Sof μ g  1/4 )-μ o  1/4 μg  1/ 4 [Eq. 410.3]

where

S of= S′o /S′ og

S gf= S′g /S′ og

S′og=S ′o+ S′g

The effective oil, gas and solvent densities (ρ o eff , ρ g eff , ρ s eff ) are now computed from the effective saturation fractions in equations 410.1 to 410.3, and the pure component densities (ρ o, ρg, ρ s) using the following formulae

ρ o eff =ρo (S oSn) oe+ ρs[ 1-( So Sn )oe ] [Eq. 410.4]
ρ g eff =ρs (S oSn) ge+ ρg[ 1-( So Sn )ge ] [Eq. 410.5]
ρ s eff =ρs (S sSn) se+ ρgSgf[1-(Ss Sn )se ]+ ρoS of[ 1-( Ss Sn )se ] [Eq. 410.6]

In the special case of no mixing, ω=0, the effective saturation fractions defined in equations 410.1 - 410.3 simplify to:

(So Sn )oe =1, (So Sn )ge =0, (Ss Sn )se =1

so that the effective densities, equations 410.4 - 410.6 are equal to their pure phase values.

Note that equations 410.1 and 410.2 are singular for unit mobility ratio solvent displacements, μo=μs or μ g=μs. In that case, the effective densities are computed as follows. First a mixture density is defined as:

ρ m=ρ o(SoS n)+ρg(Sg Sn )+ρs( Ss Sn) [Eq. 410.7]

Then

ρ o eff =(1 -ω)ρo+ ωρm [Eq. 410.8]
ρ g eff =(1 -ω)ρg+ ωρm [Eq. 410.9]

ρ s eff =(1 -ω)ρs+ ωρm

Where ω is the Todd and Longstaff parameter input with the TLMIXPAR keyword.
