Water PVT properties (ECLIPSE 100)

The surfactant modifies the viscosity of the pure or salted water input using the PVTW or the PVTWSALT keyword, respectively. The surfactant viscosity input as a function of surfactant concentration using the SURFVISC keyword is used to calculate the water-surfactant solution viscosity as follows:

μws (C surf  ,P)=μ w⁡ (P) μ S⁡(Cs urf  )μw⁡ (P ref  ) [Eq. 468.1]

If the brine option is active (and the polymer option is inactive), the previous equation becomes a function of salt concentration c s as well:

μws (C surf  ,P,cs )= μw ⁡(P, cs )⋅ μS⁡( Csurf  ) μw ⁡(Pref  , csre f) [Eq. 468.2]

where

μ S is the viscosity from the SURFVISC keyword(s)
μ w is the viscosity from the PVTW or PVTWSALT keyword
μ ws   is the viscosity of the water-surfactant mixture
P ref is the reference pressure in the PVTW or PVTWSALT keyword
c sref is the reference salt concentration in the PVTWSALT keyword.

Note that the SUMMARY keyword BEWV_SUR should be used to output the effective water viscosity due to the surfactant concentration.
