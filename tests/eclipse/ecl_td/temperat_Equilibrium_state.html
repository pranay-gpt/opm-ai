Equilibrium state

ECLIPSE 300

In ECLIPSE 300 a thermal flash is performed to determine the state. K-values are calculated from R s and Rv values as functions of pressure and temperature:

Koil= Rs +F1 +F⋅R v ⋅Rv⋅e Doil⋅ (1/T 0- 1/T)

Kgas= Rs +F1 +F⋅R v ⋅1 Rs ⋅e Dg as⋅(1/T 0- 1/T)

The temperature T0 is taken to be the initial reservoir temperature RTEMPA and the coefficients for the temperature dependence D oil and D gas are taken from equation 330.4 with default values of the acentric factors and critical temperatures given in table 299.1.

The constant F is the ratio of the molar volumes of gas to oil at surface conditions:

F=Vgas,s V oil,s= MW gas MW oil ⋅ρ oil ,s ρg as,s

Values of ρoil,s and ρ gas ,s are taken from the DENSITY keyword, while default values for the molecular weights are given in the following table.
