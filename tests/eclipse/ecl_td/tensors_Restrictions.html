Restrictions

The MPFA discretizations cannot at present be used with:

  • The parallel option (keyword PARALLEL)
  • Velocity dependent relative permeabilities (keyword VELDEP)
  • Cell coarsening (keyword COARSEN)
  • The Diffusion option (keyword DIFFUSE)
  • The Thermal option (keyword THERMAL)
  • Radial geometry (keyword RADIAL)
  • The WARP solver (keyword WARP)
