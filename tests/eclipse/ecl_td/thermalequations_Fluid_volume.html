Fluid volume

The specific volume of each phase can be determined from user-defined molar densities for each phase ( bo, b g and bw):

Vo =mtL bo, Vg =mtV bg, Vw =mtW bw, [Eq. 314.1]

The volume balance residual equation 310.3 can then be written as:

Rv=Vp -V p⋅(Vo +V g+ Vw)=0 [Eq. 314.2]

The phase saturations So, S g and Sw are defined as

So =Vo Vo +V g+ Vw ,S g= Vg V o+ Vg +Vw, Sw =Vw Vo +V g+ Vw , [Eq. 314.3]

When equation 314.2 holds, the phase saturations are equal to the phase specific volumes. Therefore

So =mtL bo Sg =m tV bg S w= mt Wbw [Eq. 314.4]

The number of moles of each component equation 311.1 and the energy density, equation 313.4 can now be written in terms of the saturations:

mc⁢ ⁢=  So ⋅bo⋅xoc ⁢+  Sg ⋅b g⋅xcg ⁢+  Sw ⋅b w⋅xwc [Eq. 314.5]
Vb⋅e⁢= Vp⋅(S o⋅ bo⋅eo ⁢+  Sg ⋅b g⋅eg+ Sw ⋅b w⋅ew)   +  (V b⁢−  V p)⋅ er [Eq. 314.6]

These two equations can be used for initializing the simulation - when the primary solution variables need to be calculated from user-defined saturations.
