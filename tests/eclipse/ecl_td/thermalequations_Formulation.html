Formulation

The equations used to describe thermal processes are similar to those used in the compositional simulation, "Formulation of the equations", but with three important differences: the addition of an energy variable and an energy equation; the presence of a water component in the gas phase as well as the water phase; and temperature dependence of properties.

The thermal simulator does not use an equation of state to determine the thermodynamic properties. Instead you must supply K-values to determine equilibrium and densities, viscosities and enthalpies for each component in each phase.
