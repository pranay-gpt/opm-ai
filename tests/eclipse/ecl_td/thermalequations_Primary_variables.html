Primary variables

The primary solution variables, used for cells in all phase states, are

X= (P,m1,…, mN, mw,e)

where

P is the pressure,
m 1,…, mN are molar densities of each hydrocarbon component,
m w is the molar density of the water component, and
e is the bulk internal energy density.

The molar densities of the fluid components are measured as moles per unit reservoir volume, while the energy density is measured as energy per unit bulk volume. The bulk volume includes both the reservoir volume and the rock volume.
