Thermal flash

The four flash variables can be found from the primary variables by solving the four flash equations in each cell:

∑c x oc = 1 if L>0 [Eq. 313.1]
∑c x gc +x gw    =1 i f V>0 [Eq. 313.2]
L+ V+W=1 [Eq. 313.3]

and

Vb⋅e=V p⋅ mt ⋅(L⋅ eo +V⋅e g+W⋅e w)+ (V b- Vp )⋅e r [Eq. 313.4]

where

e o, eg and e w are the user-defined molar energies of the oil, gas and water phases, and
e r is the user-defined energy per unit volume of rock.
