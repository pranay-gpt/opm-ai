Total mole fractions

The total mole fractions, which are used in equations 311.5 and 311.6 are defined as

zc= mc mT       z w=mwm T

Equation 311.2 can be written in terms of the mole fractions

∑c z c+ zw=1 [Eq. 312.1]

The total mole fractions differ from the hydrocarbon mole fractions:

ζ c=mcm T-m w= zc 1-zw [Eq. 312.2]

Substituting this into equation 312.1 gives

∑c ζ c= 1 [Eq. 312.3]
