Following the tracers

Tracer variables are concentrations, which normally lie in the range 0 to 1. Each tracer is associated with a carrier component. The equation defining the change in the tracer solution over a timestep is then:

Ri=Mi T+ΔT⋅ Ci T+ΔT- Mi TC iT +ΔTΣ n Fi→ nC inT+ΔT +ΔTC iT+ ΔT QMi =0 [Eq. 697.1]

where:

M i is the moles of carrier in cell i
C i is the tracer concentration in cell i
Fi→n is molar carrier flow from cell i to neighboring cell n
Q Mi is the carrier molar production from well completion in block i
C in is the upstreamed tracer concentration from cell i to neighboring cell n.
Note: The tracer conservation equation is linear in C, and thus solves in one iteration of Newton’s method.
