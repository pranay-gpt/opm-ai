Azimuthal transmissibility

The azimuthal transmissibility is given by:

TRANTi =CDARCY ⋅TML TTi⋅D IPC 1Ti +1 Tj [Eq. 18.1]

with

Ti=2⋅ PERMTi ⋅RNTG i⋅ DZi ⋅ln⁡(R2 /R 1) Dθ i

where

R 1 is the inner radius,
R 2 is the outer radius.
T j is similar.

It should be noted that ln() implies the natural logarithm throughout. This expression is also used in completing the circle in radial geometry.

Dip correction

The dip correction is given by:

D IPC= DHS DHS+DVS

where

DHS= (1 2( Dθi+D θj )⋅ Rmean )2

and

D VS= (DEPTHi -DEPTHj )2 .

For ECLIPSE 100, Rmean takes a value of one, and for ECLIPSE 300, Rmean represents an average of the inner and outer radius.
