R-transmissibility

TRANRi =CDARCY⋅TMLTRi⋅DIPC 1 Ti +1 Tj [Eq. 17.1]

with

Ti=PERMR i⋅ RNTGi ⋅Dθ i⋅ DZi D 1P

D1P =R 12 (R22 ⁢−  R1 2) ln⁡ (R1/R 2) +1 2

and

Tj=PERMR j⋅ RNTGj ⋅Dθ j⋅ DZj D 2M

D2M =R 32 (R32 ⁢−  R2 2) ln⁡ (R3/R 2) -1 2

where

R 1 is the inner radius of cell i,
R 2 is the outer radius of cell i,
R 3 is the outer radius of cell j.

Dip correction

The dip correction is given by:

D IPC= DHS DHS+DVS

where

DHS= (1 2( R3 -R 1))2

and

D VS= (DEPTHi -DEPTHj )2 .
