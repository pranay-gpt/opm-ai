Transmissibility calculations

x ECLIPSE 100
x ECLIPSE 300

This chapter describes the expressions used by ECLIPSE to calculate transmissibility values, between cells in the grid, and between cells and numerical aquifers.

The transmissibility calculations for each type of geometry are detailed in the next section. In the corner point case, transmissibility values between cells that are not neighbors in the basic indexing grid may be calculated on the same basis as those for neighboring cells. In both cases, the mutual interface area is calculated, and this may be non-zero for non-neighboring cells at faults.
