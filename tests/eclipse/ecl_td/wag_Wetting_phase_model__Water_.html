Wetting phase model (Water)

The wetting phase model is based on the observation that the water relative permeability curve measured following a gas flood shows significantly less mobility. An example of the two relative permeability curves is shown in the following figure.

The two-phase curves are taken to be the drainage curves (SATNUM) and the three-phase curves to be the imbibition curves (IMBNUM). Note that these curves are not strictly drainage and imbibition but apply to the two-phase and three-phase cases respectively.
