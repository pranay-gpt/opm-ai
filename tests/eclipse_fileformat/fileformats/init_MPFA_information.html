MPFA information

ECLIPSE 300 only

For the Multi-point flux approximation method, the MPFA region numbers and the 9-point forward transmissibilities will be output to the .INIT file for global cells only.

The MPFA regions numbers will be output under the header MPFANUM.

The 9-point forward transmissibilities will be written to the .INIT with the first 4 characters of the heading denoted by “MPFT”. The 5th character will denote the direction of the transmissibility, either “X” or “Y” or “Z”. The last 3 characters in the heading name (characters 6-8) describe the X, Y, and Z offsets in the natural direction to the transmissibility connection from cell (I,J,K) to cell (I+IXOFF, J+JYOFF, K+KZOFF) in the external direction. For example, an offset of -1 will be denoted by the single character “M”, an offset of 0 will be denoted by the single character “O” and an offset of +1 will be denoted by the single character “P”. So, output to the .INIT file could contain headings such as “MPFTXPOM” or “MPFTZPOP” for instance.
