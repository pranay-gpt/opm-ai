File Format Example

Assume a water oil case with three streamlines, as seen in Figure 61.1 for report step 1. Two streamlines starts at the injector "INJ1" and terminate in producer "PROD1". The third streamline starts at a boundary condition "PSIDE" and terminates in "PROD1". The date for the report step is the 20th October 1997 and the time of the report step is 23:50:41.
